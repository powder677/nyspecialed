# What Is an IEP in NYC District 24 - Middle Village / Ridgewood?

An Individualized Education Program (IEP) is a legally binding document that outlines the specialized instruction and support services your child with disabilities is entitled to receive at no cost to your family. In NYC District 24, serving the diverse neighborhoods of Middle Village and Ridgewood, IEPs ensure that children with learning differences, developmental delays, autism, ADHD, and other disabilities can access the curriculum alongside their peers while receiving the targeted support they need to succeed.

For families in District 24's tight-knit communities—where nearly 40% of residents speak a language other than English at home and many families have deep roots spanning generations—understanding your rights and the IEP process is crucial for advocating effectively for your child. The IEP serves as your child's educational roadmap, detailing specific goals, accommodations, modifications, and related services that will help them make meaningful progress in school and prepare for their future.

## The IEP Process in NYC District 24 - Middle Village / Ridgewood

The Committee on Special Education (CSE) process in NYC District 24 follows the same citywide procedures while serving the unique needs of our community's diverse student population. Here's how the process works:

**Initial Referral**: Parents, teachers, or other professionals can refer a child for special education evaluation. In District 24's multilingual community, interpretation services are available in languages including Spanish, Chinese, Italian, and Polish to ensure all families can participate meaningfully.

**Evaluation**: The district conducts a comprehensive evaluation at no cost, which may include psychological, educational, speech-language, occupational therapy, and other assessments as needed. Evaluations are conducted by licensed professionals and must be completed within 60 school days of consent.

**Eligibility Determination**: The CSE team reviews evaluation results to determine if your child has a disability that impacts their educational performance and requires special education services.

**IEP Development**: If eligible, the CSE team develops an individualized program with measurable goals, appropriate services, and placement recommendations. The team considers your child's strengths, needs, and how their disability affects their involvement in the general curriculum.

**Implementation and Review**: Once you consent to the IEP, the district must implement it immediately. IEPs are reviewed annually, and your child is reevaluated every three years (or sooner if needed).

## Who Attends the IEP Meeting

Your child's CSE team includes these required members:

• **You** as the parent or guardian
• A general education teacher (if your child participates in general education)
• A special education teacher or provider
• A school psychologist or district representative who can interpret evaluation results
• A district representative authorized to commit school resources
• Related services providers (when appropriate)
• Your child (when appropriate, especially for transition planning)

As a parent, you have the right to bring advocates, request interpreters, and invite anyone with knowledge or special expertise about your child to the meeting.

## Your Rights as a Parent in NYC District 24 - Middle Village / Ridgewood

Under IDEA and New York State Education Law, you have comprehensive rights including:

**Prior Written Notice**: The district must inform you in writing before proposing or refusing to change your child's identification, evaluation, placement, or services. All notices must be in your native language or preferred mode of communication.

**Consent Requirements**: Your written consent is required before initial evaluations, initial provision of services, and reevaluations. You can revoke consent for services at any time.

**Access to Records**: You have the right to inspect and review all educational records related to your child, request copies, and have records explained to you.

**Independent Educational Evaluation**: If you disagree with the district's evaluation, you can request an independent evaluation at public expense.

**Dispute Resolution**: You can resolve disagreements through mediation, due process hearings, or State complaints. These services are free and available in your preferred language.

**Stay-Put Provision**: During disputes, your child continues receiving their current services until the matter is resolved.

## Common IEP Services Available in NYC

District 24 schools provide a full range of special education services including:

• **Speech-Language Therapy**: Addresses communication disorders, including articulation, language processing, and social communication skills
• **Occupational Therapy**: Helps with fine motor skills, sensory processing, and activities of daily living needed for school success
• **Physical Therapy**: Supports gross motor skills, mobility, and physical function
• **Integrated Co-Teaching (ICT)**: Two teachers (general and special education) work together in an inclusive classroom
• **Special Class in a Community School**: Self-contained classes for students needing more intensive support
• **Counseling Services**: Individual or group counseling to address social-emotional needs
• **Vision or Hearing Support**: Specialized services for students with sensory impairments
• **Assistive Technology**: Devices and services to support communication, learning, and independence

## Next Steps for Parents

1. **Document your concerns** by keeping detailed records of your child's academic struggles, behaviors, and any communications with school staff.

2. **Request an evaluation in writing** if you suspect your child may have a disability affecting their educational performance.

3. **Prepare for meetings** by reviewing your child's records, writing down questions, and considering what services might help your child succeed.

4. **Know your timeline** because the district has specific deadlines for completing evaluations and developing IEPs.

5. **Stay engaged** by maintaining regular communication with your child's team and monitoring progress toward IEP goals.
