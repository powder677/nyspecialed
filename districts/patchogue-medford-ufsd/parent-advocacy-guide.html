At the Table: How to Advocate Effectively Without Burning Bridges

Participate actively in CSE meetings. Clearly articulate your concerns and share your observations about your child's needs. Bring data and specific examples to support your requests.

Keep a record of everything discussed and agreed upon at the meeting. Request a copy of the finalized IEP promptly after the meeting.

Under New York State law, Patchogue-Medford UFSD must complete an initial evaluation within 60 school days of receiving parental consent. What are my rights to an Independent Educational Evaluation (IEE) in Patchogue-Medford UFSD?

If you disagree with an evaluation conducted by Patchogue-Medford UFSD, you have the right to request an IEE at public expense. The district can either agree to pay for the IEE or initiate an impartial hearing to demonstrate that its evaluation was appropriate. If the hearing officer determines the district's evaluation was appropriate, you still have the right to an IEE, but at your own expense.
