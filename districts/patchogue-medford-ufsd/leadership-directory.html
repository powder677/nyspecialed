Navigating the special education system can be overwhelming, so knowing who to contact within the district is crucial. This page provides key contacts within Patchogue-Medford UFSD who can assist you with your child's IEP and special education services.

Key Roles in Patchogue-Medford UFSD's Special Education Department

CSE Chairperson
Contact the CSE Chairperson for all matters related to your child's IEP, evaluations, and CSE meetings.
Director of Special Education
Reach out to the Director of Special Education for district-wide special education policies and procedures.
Building Principal
Contact the Building Principal for concerns specific to your child's school and their implementation of IEP services.
School Psychologist
The School Psychologist can provide insights into your child's evaluations, testing results, and social-emotional well-being.
Special Education Teacher
Your child's special education teacher is your point of contact for daily classroom support, academic progress, and IEP goal implementation.

How to Reach District Staff Effectively

When contacting Patchogue-Medford UFSD staff, it's best to communicate in writing, keeping copies of all correspondence for your records. If you don't receive a timely response, consider sending a follow-up email or letter, or utilizing a formal Freedom of Information Law (FOIL) request for relevant documents.

When the District Doesn't Respond — NYSED Complaints

If you've exhausted all avenues within the district and believe Patchogue-Medford UFSD is not meeting its obligations, you have the right to file a formal complaint with the New York State Education Department's (NYSED) Office of Special Education.
