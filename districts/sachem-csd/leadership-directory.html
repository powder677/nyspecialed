Knowing who to contact within the Sachem Central School District is the first step in advocating for your child's special education needs. Understanding the roles of different staff members can help you resolve issues and access the support your child deserves.

Key Roles in Sachem CSD's Special Education Department

CSE Chairperson
Contact the CSE Chairperson for all matters related to your child's IEP, including initial evaluations, annual reviews, and IEP modifications.
Director of Special Education
The Director of Special Education oversees all special education programs and services within the Sachem Central School District; contact them for district-wide policy concerns.
Building Principal
Your child's Building Principal is responsible for ensuring that the IEP is implemented effectively within the school building; contact them for school-level concerns.
School Psychologist
The School Psychologist can provide support and guidance regarding your child's emotional and behavioral needs, as well as offer insights into psychological evaluations.
Transition Coordinator
Contact the Transition Coordinator to begin planning for your child's post-secondary education, employment, and independent living skills as they approach adulthood.

How to Reach District Staff Effectively

Always communicate with Sachem CSD staff in writing, keeping copies of all emails and letters, and consider submitting a formal Freedom of Information Law (FOIL) request if you require specific documents or information.

When the District Doesn't Respond — NYSED Complaints

If you are unable to resolve your concerns with the district directly, you have the right to file a formal complaint with the New York State Education Department's (NYSED) Office of Special Education.
