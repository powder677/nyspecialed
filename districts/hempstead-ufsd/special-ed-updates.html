What's Happening in Hempstead UFSD Special Education Right Now

Hempstead Union Free School District (UFSD) faces ongoing challenges in providing adequate special education services, particularly regarding timely evaluations and mandated services. Recent data suggests a backlog in initial evaluations exceeding the NYSED's 60-day timeframe, raising concerns about compliance with Part 200 Regulations. This delay can significantly impact a student's access to appropriate support and individualized education programs (IEPs).

Systemic issues within Hempstead UFSD's special education department may contribute to these challenges. Reports indicate a high turnover rate among special education teachers and related service providers (e.g., speech therapists, occupational therapists, psychologists), potentially stemming from workload concerns and lack of administrative support. This instability can disrupt the continuity of services for students with disabilities and hinder the implementation of effective IEPs.

Furthermore, Hempstead UFSD must address concerns regarding the accessibility and quality of IEPs. Parents have voiced concerns about the lack of meaningful parent participation in the CSE process and the insufficient individualization of IEP goals. These issues are frequently raised during mediations and impartial hearings, potentially leading to costly legal settlements for the district if not addressed proactively through improved communication and collaborative IEP development.

IEP & CSE Timeline Red Flags in NY State

Under NY State law (Part 200 Regulations), once you sign consent for an initial evaluation, the district has 60 calendar days to evaluate your child and hold the Committee on Special Education (CSE) meeting to determine eligibility. These timelines are legally binding.

One Action Step You Can Take Today

Send a written, dated request via email to your campus principal AND the Hempstead UFSD Special Education Office requesting a Full Evaluation if you have concerns. Verbal requests do not start the legal clock in New York.

Frequently Asked Questions: Hempstead UFSD Special Education

Real questions New York parents are searching for — answered with NYSED regulations in mind.

How do I request a special education evaluation for my child in Hempstead UFSD?

To request a special education evaluation, submit a written request to the principal of your child's school or directly to the district's Committee on Special Education (CSE). The request should clearly state your concerns about your child's academic, social, emotional, or physical development and why you believe a special education evaluation is necessary. Be sure to retain a copy of your request for your records.

What happens after I request a special education evaluation in Hempstead UFSD?

Within 10 school days of receiving your written request, the Hempstead UFSD CSE must provide you with a consent form. Once you provide consent for the evaluation, the district has 60 calendar days (excluding July and August) to complete the evaluation and hold a CSE meeting to determine your child's eligibility for special education services. During this time, the district will conduct various assessments to understand your child's strengths and areas of need.

What if I disagree with the results of the special education evaluation conducted by Hempstead UFSD?

If you disagree with the findings of the Hempstead UFSD's evaluation, you have the right to obtain an Independent Educational Evaluation (IEE) at public expense if you can demonstrate that the district's evaluation was not appropriate. You must notify the district of your intention to seek an IEE. The district may either agree to fund the IEE or initiate an impartial hearing to defend the appropriateness of its evaluation. If the Impartial Hearing Officer (IHO) determines the district's evaluation was appropriate, you will not be entitled to reimbursement for the IEE.

What services are available for students with IEPs in Hempstead UFSD?

Hempstead UFSD offers a range of special education services, including but not limited to: Special Education Teacher Support Services (SETSS), Resource Room, Integrated Co-Teaching (ICT), Special Class, speech therapy, occupational therapy, physical therapy, counseling, and assistive technology. The specific services your child receives will be determined by the CSE based on their individual needs as outlined in their IEP.

How often is my child's IEP reviewed in Hempstead UFSD?

Your child's IEP must be reviewed at least annually by the CSE. However, you have the right to request a CSE meeting at any time if you have concerns about your child's progress or the effectiveness of their IEP. It's recommended to document your concerns in writing and submit them to the CSE chairperson to formally request a meeting.

What can I do if I feel my child's IEP is not being implemented properly in Hempstead UFSD?

If you believe your child's IEP is not being implemented as written, first contact your child's teacher, special education teacher, or the school principal to discuss your concerns. Document all communication. If the issue is not resolved, you can contact the CSE chairperson and request a meeting to address the implementation concerns. If the problem persists, you may consider mediation or filing an impartial hearing request to seek resolution.
