Understanding Special Education in Greece CSD

Greece Central School District parents have the right to access free appropriate public education (FAPE) for their children. This includes advocating for the least restrictive environment (LRE), independent educational evaluations (IEE) at public expense when warranted, and comprehensive transition planning services.
