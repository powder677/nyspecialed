Connecting with the appropriate staff at Greece CSD is crucial for addressing your child's special education needs. Start by reaching out to school-based staff, and then district-level administrators, if necessary.

Key Roles in Greece CSD's Special Education Department

Building Principal
Contact the building principal as the primary point of contact for all school-related concerns, including special education issues.
School Psychologist
Reach out to the school psychologist for concerns regarding your child's academic, social, or emotional well-being, as they play a key role in evaluations and IEP development.
CSE Chairperson
Contact the CSE Chairperson to schedule IEP meetings, discuss evaluation results, and address concerns regarding your child's special education program.
Director of Special Education
Contact the Director of Special Education for district-wide special education policies, procedures, and concerns that cannot be resolved at the school level.
504 Coordinator
Contact the 504 Coordinator to address concerns related to accommodations for students with disabilities who may not require special education services.

How to Reach District Staff Effectively

When contacting Greece CSD staff, it is advisable to communicate in writing and keep a record of all correspondence, including dates, times, and the content of your conversations. Consider utilizing Freedom of Information Law (FOIL) requests to obtain relevant documentation.

When the District Doesn't Respond — NYSED Complaints

If you are unable to resolve your concerns with the district, you have the right to file a formal complaint with the New York State Education Department's (NYSED) Office of Special Education.
