Navigating special education in East Ramapo CSD can be challenging; understanding who to contact and when is crucial. Start by reaching out to the CSE chairperson or your child's school psychologist with any initial concerns or questions.

Key Roles in East Ramapo CSD's Special Education Department

CSE Chairperson
Contact the CSE Chairperson for all IEP-related matters, including initial evaluations, annual reviews, and program modifications.
Director of Special Education
Contact the Director of Special Education for district-level concerns regarding special education services or policy implementation.
Assistant Superintendent for Pupil Services
Reach out to the Assistant Superintendent for Pupil Services for issues related to student support services and overall educational programs.
Building Special Education Coordinator
Contact the Building Special Education Coordinator for school-specific questions or problems related to your child's special education program.
School Psychologist
Contact the School Psychologist for psychological evaluations, counseling services, and support regarding your child's social and emotional well-being.

How to Reach District Staff Effectively

Document all communication with district staff in writing, including dates, times, and a summary of the conversation. Consider submitting a Freedom of Information Law (FOIL) request to obtain relevant documents and information regarding your child's special education records.

When the District Doesn't Respond — NYSED Complaints

If you are unable to resolve concerns with East Ramapo CSD despite repeated attempts, you have the right to file a formal complaint with the New York State Education Department (NYSED) Office of Special Education.
