Our Methodology

How we research, build, and maintain this resource.

How We Select Districts

We prioritize school districts in New York State based on enrollment size, demonstrated parent demand, and known concentrations of special education dispute activity. For New York City, we maintain pages for each of the 32 community school districts plus District 75. Statewide, we cover the Big 5 cities and the largest suburban districts across Long Island, Westchester, and upstate regions.

How We Research Content

Content on each district page is based on:

  • NYSED official guidance — Part 200 regulations, IDEA compliance memos, and state complaint decisions
  • NYSED State Review Office (SRO) decisions — publicly available impartial hearing decisions that reveal district-specific patterns
  • District-level data — from NYSED's data portal, including IEP rates, evaluation completion rates, and placement data
  • Community context — demographics, language access needs, and known advocacy issues in each district

How We Vet Provider Listings

Attorneys, advocates, and evaluators can apply to be listed on district partner pages. We do not list providers without request and payment. Before approving a listing we verify:

  • Bar admission or licensure status where applicable
  • Stated service area includes the listed district
  • No active disciplinary proceedings at time of listing

Listing does not constitute endorsement. We recommend families independently verify credentials and speak with any provider before engaging their services.

How We Stay Current

We review and update content when NYSED issues new guidance, when state law changes, or when district-specific information becomes outdated. If you see an error, let us know — parents helping parents is the whole point.

What We Don't Do

We do not contact school districts for comment before publishing. We do not accept payment from school districts or district consultants. We do not remove factually accurate information about district practices at district request.
