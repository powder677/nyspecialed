Privacy Policy for newyorkspecialed.net

newyorkspecialed.net ("we," "our," or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit and use newyorkspecialed.net (our "Website"). By accessing or using our Website, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect both Personal Information and Non-Personal Information from you.

1.1. Personal Information

Personal Information is data that can identify you. We collect this when you voluntarily provide it, such as:

  • Contact Information: Your name, email address, phone number, or mailing address when you submit forms, subscribe to newsletters, or communicate with us.
  • Correspondence: Records of your communications with us.

1.2. Non-Personal Information

Non-Personal Information cannot identify you personally and is collected automatically as you use our Website. This includes:

  • Usage Data: Your IP address, browser type, operating system, device type, pages visited, time spent, and referring URLs.
  • Cookie Data: Information gathered via cookies and tracking technologies (see Section 3).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for purposes such as:

  • Providing Services: To operate, maintain, and enhance our Website and educational resources.
  • Responding to Inquiries: To address your questions and requests.
  • Sending Communications: To send newsletters, updates, or administrative messages you've opted into. You can opt-out of marketing communications at any time.
  • Improving User Experience: To understand user engagement and personalize your experience.
  • Analytics: To monitor and analyze usage trends for Website improvement.
  • Security: To detect, prevent, and address technical issues or illegal activities.
  • Legal Compliance: To meet applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our Website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your experience and gather usage information.

3.1. What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website. They help websites function efficiently and provide data to website owners.

3.2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for:

  • Essential Functionality: Strictly necessary for our Website's operation.
  • Performance & Analytics: We use Google Analytics cookies to understand how users interact with our Website. Information (including your IP address) is transmitted to Google and used to evaluate Website usage and compile activity reports.
  • Functionality: To remember your preferences and provide personalized features.

3.3. Your Choices Regarding Cookies

You can accept or decline cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers accept cookies by default, but you can usually modify settings to decline them. Disabling cookies may impact Website functionality.

4. Disclosure of Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your Personal Information to third parties for their direct marketing. We may share your information in these situations:

  • Service Providers: With third-party providers for website hosting, analytics, email delivery, etc. These parties are contractually bound to protect your information.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, court order, or governmental requests.
  • Protection of Rights: To protect our rights, property, or safety, or those of our users or others.
  • Business Transfers: During a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred.
  • Aggregated Data: We may share anonymized, non-personal data for research or analysis.

5. Third-Party Links

Our Website may link to third-party websites not controlled by newyorkspecialed.net. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these external sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your Personal Information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. These include SSL/TLS encryption, system monitoring, and restricted data access. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Our Website is for adults (parents, educators, professionals). We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe your child has provided us with Personal Information, please contact us at privacy@newyorkspecialed.net , and we will remove it.

8. Your Rights and Choices

You may have the following rights regarding your Personal Information:

  • Access & Correction: Request access to, or correction of, your data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your Personal Information (subject to legal exceptions).
  • Opt-Out of Communications: Unsubscribe from marketing emails via provided instructions or by contacting us.
  • Cookie Preferences: Manage through your browser settings.
  • "Do Not Track" Signals: We do not currently respond to DNT signals due to a lack of universal standards.

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information in Section 9.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will post the new policy on this page and update the "Effective Date." We encourage you to review this policy regularly for changes. Updates are effective when posted.

10. Contact Us

For questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:

Email: privacy@newyorkspecialed.net
